Asosiy atamalar
  • ping — ICMP echo bilan ulanish borligini tekshiradi
  • tracert — paket o‘tgan routerlar yo‘lini ko‘rsatadi
  • TTL — paket umri, har bir routerda 1 ga kamayadi
  • debugging — tizim ishini batafsil kuzatish rejimi

1. ping — ulanishni tekshirish

ICMP ustida ishlaydi: manba echo request, manzil echo reply.

  • javob keldi — manzilning IP qatlami ishlayapti
  • javob yo‘q — destination unreachable yoki timeout

5. tracert mexanizmi

  • TTL 1 dan boshlanadi va har so‘rovda 1 ga oshadi
  • har router TTL ni 1 ga kamaytiradi; 0 bo‘lsa — TTL exceeded
  • manzilga yetsa — port unreachable, izlash to‘xtaydi
  • shu yo‘l bilan har hopning IP manzili aniqlanadi

8. debugging ip packet natijasi

4-rasm. Bitta ping uchta qayd beradi: chiqish · kelish · yetkazish

Xulosa

  • ping = ICMP echo request + echo reply
  • ping standarti: 5 so‘rov · 56 bayt · timeout 2000 ms
  • tracert = TTL ni oshirib borish; TTL exceeded → har hop bilinadi
  • tracert standarti: maks TTL 30 · 3 sinov · UDP 33434
  • debug chiqishi: protokol kaliti va ekran kaliti — ikkisi ham kerak

Tekshirish

  1. ping qaysi protokolda? — ICMP (protocol 1)
  2. tracert nimadan foydalanadi? — TTL tugashi
  3. tracert maks TTL sukutda? — 30
  4. Oraliq router nima qaytaradi? — TTL exceeded, type 11
  5. debug ko‘rinishi uchun nima kerak? — ikki kalit ON
