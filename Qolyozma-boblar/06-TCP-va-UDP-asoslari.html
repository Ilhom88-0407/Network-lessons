TCP va UDP asoslari TCP and UDP Fundamentals

Asosiy atamalar
  • TCP — ulanishli, ishonchli transport protokoli (RFC 793)
  • UDP — ulanishsiz, tasdiqsiz, tez protokol (RFC 768)
  • Port — bitta xostdagi ilovalarni ajratuvchi raqam
  • Segment — transport qatlami uzatadigan ma’lumot bo‘lagi

1. Transport qatlami vazifalari

Ilova va IP orasida — xostdan xostga ulanish.

  • ulanishli / ulanishsiz xizmat
  • holatni saqlash — ulanishlar bazasi
  • segmentlash — oqimni bo‘laklarga bo‘lish
  • multiplekslash — port bilan ilovani ajratish
  • ishonchlilik — faqat TCP
  • oqim nazorati — tezlikni moslash

1-rasm. Bir IP manzil — ko‘p ilova, portlar ajratadi

Xulosa

  • Transport qatlami: port · segment · oqim · ishonchlilik
  • Port 16 bit; 0–1023 mashhur; TCP=6, UDP=17
  • Ochish — 3 qadam (SYN), yopish — 4 qadam (FIN)
  • Seq va Ack: tartib, yo‘qolish, takror
  • Oyna ≤ 65535 bayt; Win=0 — to‘xtash
  • UDP: 8 bayt, tasdiq yo‘q, kechikish kichik
